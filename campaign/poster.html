Ⅱ Pause / Student safety

Your account.
Your control.

A part-time job should not need your OTP, banking password or bank card.

Before you accept

  • Pause if asked to receive and move someone else’s money.
  • Find the employer’s official contact yourself.
  • Ask for written duties and payment terms.
  • Keep banking access private.

Sebelum anda terima

  • Berhenti jika diminta menerima dan memindahkan wang orang lain.
  • Cari sendiri saluran rasmi majikan.
  • Minta tugas dan terma bayaran bertulis.
  • Rahsiakan akses perbankan.
